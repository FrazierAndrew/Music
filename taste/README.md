# taste/: how Andrew hears music

What he likes, what he rejects, and the evidence. Claude cannot listen, so this folder is the closest thing
to ears the repo has: read it before planning any render.

| File | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| [`profile.md`](profile.md) | Standing preferences, each with its source. Changes rarely. |
| [`feedback-log.md`](feedback-log.md) | Every reaction to every render, dated, quoted, newest last. Append only. |

Rules:

- Quote him. Keep his words separate from our reading of them ("Said" vs "Read as" in the log).
- One reaction is a data point, not a rule. Promote a pattern into `profile.md` when it repeats or when he
  states it as a rule, and cite the log entries.
- Keep rejects and the renders he did not pick: what he passes over is as informative as what he likes.
- Ask for timestamps ("what happens at 1:45 for you?"): they tie a reaction to a section and a recipe line.
- Only music taste lives here. Nothing personal beyond that.
