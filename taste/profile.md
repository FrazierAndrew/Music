# Taste profile

Standing preferences for everything this repo makes. Sources: a date means he said it in that session (see
`feedback-log.md`); "earlier chats" means he stated it in earlier conversations with Claude.

## Non-negotiables

- Electronic music only. (earlier chats)
- No Suno, no Ableton: custom code workflows with Claude, mostly remixes of tracks he already made. (2026-10-19)
- His listening beats the metrics. The checks in `craft/verification.md` catch defects; they do not decide
  what is good. (earlier chats)
- Preserve originals: never process a source file in place. (earlier chats)
- No audible pumping, flattening or unnecessary processing as side effects. (earlier chats) Pumping as a
  deliberate style move (the Flume-style chords) is fine; pumping caused by the master is not.
- A remix changes the sound design and the texture, not only the drums: "I want sound design and noise
  change. Way more creative change." (2026-10-19)

## What he values

- Impact, emotional arc, spaciousness, atmosphere, and controlled experimentation within electronic music.
  (earlier chats)
- Emotional intensity. In his track ddd2 he loves the last drop: "where that bass comes, it's like a soft
  tidal wave, so good". (2026-10-19) Spec of that moment: `tracks/ddd2/README.md`.
- "Nostalgic and sad but in a euphoric ambient sense" means contrast, space and atmosphere, not loudness.
  (earlier chats)
- Interested in underground and fringe EDM. (earlier chats)

## Direction so far (data points, not rules yet)

- All seven renders up to `ddd2_flume_ambient` were "garbage" to him (2026-10-20): the Flume-style preference below was relative, not approval.
- Electronic only, so lyrics never matter; vocal-like sounds are texture at most. (2026-10-20)
- Recipes that kept his audio and changed drums and arrangement (ddd2 A/B/C) were not enough. (2026-10-19)
- Of two style engines he picked the Flume-style one (deconstructed, dirty, wonky, metallic, off-grid) over
  the ODESZA-style one (organic, cinematic, vocal chops, brass), and asked for an ambient version of it.
  (2026-10-20)

## Moves he already uses on his own tracks

Tape slowdown, filter bloom, hall reverb, granular shimmer, vinyl crackle, paulstretch ending (earlier
chats). An earlier ambient edit of one of his songs used breathing, memory layers, reverse swells, stereo
bloom, shimmer and a long tail (earlier chats). All of these exist in `engine/` (see `craft/toolkit.md`).

## Mastering

- Reference: Spotify playback, with Flume as the sound reference. (earlier chats)
- Loudness in EBU R128 / LUFS. (earlier chats)
- Prefers hands-off, automated mastering. (earlier chats)

## Working with him

- Comfortable with code, not a DSP specialist: explain technically, without assuming DSP background.
  (earlier chats)
- Concise and honest; correct him when he is wrong; ask when a request is ambiguous. (earlier chats)
- No emojis and no em dashes, anywhere. (earlier chats)
- Act as a team member: bring ideas and objections, not only literal execution. (earlier chats)
- For taste learning he keeps every candidate including rejects, with exact settings, timestamped
  reactions and pairwise comparisons (from his own taste-dataset work, earlier chats). This repo follows
  that: `renders/README.md` keeps every render and `feedback-log.md` every reaction.
